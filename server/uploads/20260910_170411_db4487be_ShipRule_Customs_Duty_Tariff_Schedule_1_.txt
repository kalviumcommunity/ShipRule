SHIPRULE
CUSTOMS DUTY & TARIFF SCHEDULE
Knowledge Base Document

PURPOSE
This document provides a structured reference for answering questions about Indian import customs duties and tariff schedules. It covers Basic Customs Duty (BCD), effective/concessional BCD, Social Welfare Surcharge (SWS), Integrated GST (IGST), Anti-Dumping Duty (ADD), and other applicable customs duties.

IMPORTANT
Customs duty rates are product-specific and can change based on tariff classification, country of origin, importer/end use, effective dates, exemptions, preferential trade agreements, and government notifications.

ShipRule should never assume that one fixed duty rate applies to all products. For an accurate answer, identify the applicable HS/CTH classification and verify the effective tariff/notification.

1. BASIC CUSTOMS DUTY (BCD)

Basic Customs Duty is the primary customs duty applicable to imported goods under the Indian Customs Tariff framework.

BCD is determined primarily by:
- Indian Customs Tariff Heading (CTH/HS Code)
- Product description and technical characteristics
- Applicable tariff item
- Effective customs notifications
- Country of origin where preferential treatment applies
- Eligible exemptions or concessional-rate notifications
- Date of import

SHIPRULE RULE:
Always distinguish between the standard tariff BCD rate and the effective BCD rate after applicable exemptions, concessions, or notifications.

Recommended fields:
- hs_code
- cth
- product_description
- bcd_tariff_rate
- bcd_effective_rate
- bcd_notification
- effective_from
- effective_to
- origin_country

2. SOCIAL WELFARE SURCHARGE (SWS)

Social Welfare Surcharge is an additional customs surcharge applicable to specified imports.

SWS should not be treated as a simple percentage of the invoice value. Its calculation base depends on the applicable customs provisions and exclusions.

SHIPRULE RULE:
Store SWS separately from BCD and calculate it using the legally applicable base.

Recommended fields:
- sws_rate
- sws_base_rule
- sws_exemption
- sws_notification

3. INTEGRATED GST (IGST) ON IMPORTS

IGST is collected on imported goods under the customs framework. The applicable IGST rate depends on the classification and applicable GST/tax schedule.

The IGST calculation base is not necessarily the invoice value alone. The legally prescribed import-tax base must be used.

SHIPRULE RULE:
Never calculate IGST as simply:
Assessable Value × IGST Rate

Instead:
1. Determine the assessable value.
2. Determine applicable customs duties/components.
3. Determine the legally applicable IGST base.
4. Apply the correct IGST rate.
5. Check exemptions or special treatment.

Recommended fields:
- igst_rate
- igst_base_rule
- igst_exemption
- igst_notification

4. ANTI-DUMPING DUTY (ADD)

Anti-Dumping Duty is a trade-remedy duty imposed on specified imported products where an applicable anti-dumping measure is in force.

ADD is highly specific and may depend on:
- Product
- Technical/product description
- HS/CTH classification
- Country of origin
- Country of export
- Exporter
- Producer
- Quantity/unit of measurement
- Applicable notification
- Effective date
- Product exclusions

SHIPRULE RULE:
Do not assume a universal Anti-Dumping Duty percentage.

An ADD lookup should match:
Product + HS/CTH + Country of Origin + Exporter/Producer (where applicable) + Date

ADD may be expressed as:
- Percentage of value
- Fixed amount per unit
- Other notification-defined method

Recommended fields:
- add_applicable
- add_rate
- add_unit
- add_origin_country
- add_exporter
- add_producer
- add_notification
- add_effective_from
- add_effective_to

5. OTHER CUSTOMS DUTIES TO CHECK

Depending on the product and transaction, ShipRule should also check:

A. Safeguard Duty
A temporary trade-remedy duty that may apply to specified goods under an applicable safeguard measure.

B. Countervailing Duty (CVD)
A trade-remedy duty that may apply to subsidized imports from specified countries.

C. Agriculture Infrastructure and Development Cess (AIDC)
May apply to specified products according to the relevant customs/tax provisions.

D. Other Cesses or Customs Components
Any additional levy specifically applicable to the tariff item or notification should be considered.

E. Preferential Duty
A lower customs duty may apply when the goods qualify under an applicable Free Trade Agreement, Preferential Trade Agreement, or other preferential arrangement.

F. Exemption / Concessional Duty
A notification may provide a reduced or zero duty rate subject to specific conditions.

6. INFORMATION REQUIRED FOR AN ACCURATE TARIFF ANSWER

For the most accurate result, ShipRule should collect:

1. HS Code / CTH
Preferably the complete 8-digit Indian tariff item.

2. Product Description
Include:
- Product name
- Material/composition
- Function
- Technical specifications
- Model
- Intended use
when classification depends on these factors.

3. Country of Origin
Important for:
- Preferential rates
- Anti-Dumping Duty
- Safeguard measures
- Other trade remedies

4. Assessable Value
The customs-assessed value used for duty calculation.

5. Import Date
Duty rates and notifications are time-sensitive.

6. End Use / Importer Eligibility
Important when an exemption or concessional notification contains conditions.

7. Exporter / Producer
Important for certain anti-dumping and trade-remedy measures.

7. DUTY CALCULATION FLOW

ShipRule should use the following process:

STEP 1 — CLASSIFY
Determine the correct Indian Customs Tariff Heading (CTH/HS Code).

STEP 2 — IDENTIFY ORIGIN
Determine the country of origin and, when required, country of export and exporter/producer.

STEP 3 — DETERMINE EFFECTIVE BCD
Find the standard tariff BCD and then apply valid exemptions, concessions, preferential rates, or notifications.

STEP 4 — CHECK OTHER CUSTOMS DUTIES
Check:
- SWS
- AIDC
- Anti-Dumping Duty
- Safeguard Duty
- Countervailing Duty
- Other applicable cesses/duties

STEP 5 — DETERMINE IGST
Identify the correct IGST rate and legally prescribed import-tax base.

STEP 6 — CALCULATE TOTAL
Calculate each component separately and show a transparent breakdown.

STEP 7 — SHOW SOURCES
Provide notification numbers, dates, and source references whenever available.

8. ILLUSTRATIVE CALCULATION

The following is only an example and is NOT a statement of the current rate for any particular product.

Assume:
Assessable Value = INR 100,000
Illustrative BCD = 10%
Illustrative SWS = 10% of the specified BCD base
Illustrative IGST = 18%

BCD:
100,000 × 10% = INR 10,000

SWS:
10% × 10,000 = INR 1,000

Illustrative IGST base:
INR 111,000

IGST:
111,000 × 18% = INR 19,980

Illustrative total of these components:
10,000 + 1,000 + 19,980 = INR 30,980

IMPORTANT:
This arithmetic is illustrative only. Actual IGST base, BCD, SWS, ADD, and other duties must be determined from the applicable tariff item and legal notifications.

9. EXAMPLE USER QUESTIONS

Question 1:
What are the applicable BCD, IGST, Social Welfare Surcharge and Anti-Dumping Duty rates for importing a laptop under HS Code 8471 into India from China?

Question 2:
What is the current customs duty on importing a mobile phone into India? Include BCD, SWS and IGST.

Question 3:
For HS Code 8471, what is the effective BCD rate and are there any applicable exemptions?

Question 4:
Does Anti-Dumping Duty apply to this product when importing it from China? If yes, what is the applicable notification and duty amount?

Question 5:
Calculate the total import duty for a product with an assessable value of INR 100,000.

Question 6:
What customs duties apply to HS Code 8471 when imported from the United States?

Question 7:
Does an FTA or preferential tariff apply to this import based on the country of origin?

Question 8:
Show me the complete duty breakup for this product, including BCD, SWS, IGST, ADD and other applicable duties.

12. RAG / KNOWLEDGE BASE RULES FOR SHIPRULE

Rule 1:
Do not invent tariff rates.

Rule 2:
Do not provide a current rate unless the source supports that rate and its effective date.

Rule 3:
Always prefer the most specific tariff item available.

Rule 4:
When the user's product description is insufficient to determine classification, ask for the missing product details instead of guessing.

Rule 5:
For Anti-Dumping Duty, always consider product scope and country of origin/export before concluding that ADD applies.

Rule 6:
For preferential duty, verify country of origin and applicable origin requirements.

Rule 7:
For exemptions, verify all conditions before applying the exemption.

Rule 8:
Show each duty component separately rather than returning only a single total percentage.

Rule 9:
If a duty cannot be verified, clearly state that it requires verification rather than inventing a value.

Rule 10:
Preserve source references and effective dates for traceability.

14. FINAL NOTE

ShipRule is intended to provide grounded customs and shipping guidance. Tariff information is dynamic and must be tied to the relevant tariff classification, effective date, origin, notification, and applicable conditions.

For production-grade answers, every calculated duty should be traceable to the underlying tariff entry or official notification.
