Import and export shipments must comply with applicable customs procedures and destination-specific requirements.
Cross-border trade operations require accurate origin declaration, manifest validation, and statutory duty assessment
timelines.
Standard Incoterms 2020 define the division of costs and risks between buyer and seller:
- FOB (Free On Board): The seller delivers goods on board the vessel nominated by the buyer at the port of shipment.
The buyer bears all costs and risks from that point forward.
- CIF (Cost, Insurance, and Freight): The seller delivers goods on board the vessel and pays freight and minimum
insurance coverage to the destination port.
- DDP (Delivered Duty Paid): The seller fulfills the obligation to deliver when goods are placed at the disposal of the
buyer, cleared for import on arriving transport means.
Upon arrival at the destination port of entry, shipments are presented for formal customs examination. The electronic Bill
of Entry is assessed against the official customs tariff schedule. Duty payments (including Basic Customs Duty, Social
Welfare Surcharge, and Integrated GST) must be settled before out-of-charge clearance is granted by customs
authorities.
