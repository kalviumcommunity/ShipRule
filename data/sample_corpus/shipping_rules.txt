All international shipments must include accurate shipping documentation. Required documents may include a commercial invoice and packing list, certificate of origin, and transport documents such as a bill of lading or air waybill.

The commercial invoice must specify the seller, buyer, item description, harmonized tariff code (HS Code), unit value, and total invoice currency. Under-invoicing or misdeclaring product categories constitutes a regulatory violation subject to customs penalties.

Packing lists must detail carton markings, net weight, gross weight, and package dimensions to facilitate physical verification at the port of departure and destination port of entry.

For express courier consignments, simplified clearance applies for commercial samples with a declared value under standard statutory thresholds.
