**Importing Laptop Computers (HS 8471.30) into India – Quick‑Reference Guide**

| Item | What you need to provide / pay |
|------|--------------------------------|
| **Core import documents** | • **Commercial Invoice** (full value, description, HS 8471.30, country of origin) <br>• **Packing List** (dimensions, weight, carton count) <br>• **Bill of Lading / Air Waybill** (original) <br>• **Certificate of Origin** (if you are claiming any preferential tariff) <br>• **Import Declaration Form (Bill of Entry)** filed electronically on the ICEGATE portal <br>• **BIS Import Clearance (Form‑10) or BIS “Letter of Conformity”** – laptops are covered by the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) under IS/IEC 60950‑1 / IEC 62368‑1. | **Additional licences / permits** | • **Import License** – not required for general laptops, but if the unit contains wireless modules (Wi‑Fi, Bluetooth, cellular) you may need a **Wireless Equipment License** from the Ministry of Communications. <br>• **FSSAI / Health clearance** – **not required** for laptops. |
| **Customs duty (Basic Customs Duty – BCD)** | **0 %** (HS 8471.30 is listed under “Portable automatic data‑processing machines” and is duty‑free). |
| **Other levies & taxes** | • **Integrated Goods & Services Tax (IGST)** – **18 %** of the assessable value (CIF + duty). <br>• **Social Welfare Surcharge** – 10 % of the BCD (since BCD = 0, the surcharge is nil). <br>• **Additional Cesses** – none applicable to laptops as of the latest schedule (check periodically for any health‑cess updates). |
| **Assessable value for IGST** | **CIF value** (Cost + Insurance + Freight) **+** any customs duty (0 %). IGST = 18 % × CIF. |
| **Typical clearance flow** | 1. **Pre‑shipment** – obtain BIS‑IEC import certificate (or arrange testing in India). <br>2. **Arrival** – shipper provides Bill of Lading/Air Waybill, commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin. <br>3. **Importer** files the **Bill of Entry** on ICEGATE, attaches all documents, and uploads the BIS import clearance. <br>4. Customs may issue a **“Notice of Assessment”**; you pay IGST (and any other minor fees). <br>5. Upon payment, the cargo is released. |
| **Key references** | • Indian Customs Tariff (Chapter 84, HS 8471.30) – 0 % BCD. <br>• GST Council – Laptops attract 18 % IGST (GST Rate List, “Computer hardware – 18 %”). <br>• BIS Notification No. 7/2009‑C‑3 (mandatory BIS certification for laptops). <br>• Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology (MeitY) – Wireless equipment licensing guidelines. |
| **Tips / Common pitfalls** | • **BIS compliance is the “show‑stopper.”** If you ship without a valid BIS‑IEC import certificate, customs will hold the consignment. <br>• **Accurate HS coding** – a mis‑classification (e.g., using 8471.41 for “Portable digital computers”) can trigger a duty of 10 %+ IGST. <br>• **Currency conversion** – IGST is calculated on the CIF value in INR at the RBI‑published exchange rate on the day of clearance. <br>• **Preferential rates** – If your laptops are made in a country with a Free Trade Agreement (e.g., ASEAN‑India FTA), you can claim a reduced (or zero) duty *and* a reduced IGST rate (generally 12 % under the “GST on imports from FTA partners” rule). You must provide a **Certificate of Origin** and a **Letter of Credit/Invoice** indicating the preferential origin. |
| **Bottom line** | – **Documents:** Invoice, Packing List, B/L or AWB, Certificate of Origin (if using a preferential rate), Bill of Entry (ICEGATE), **BIS‑IEC import certificate** (mandatory). <br>– **Duty:** **0 % Basic Customs Duty**. <br>– **Taxes:** **18 % IGST** on CIF value (plus negligible surcharge). <br>– **Action:** Secure the BIS certification *before* the shipment lands, file the Bill of Entry promptly, and be ready to pay IGST at the time of clearance. |

> **Reminder:** Tariff and tax rates are subject to periodic revisions (usually announced in the Union Budget). Always verify the latest rates on the Indian Customs website (customsindiaonline.gov.in) and the GST portal (gst.gov.in) before shipping.
