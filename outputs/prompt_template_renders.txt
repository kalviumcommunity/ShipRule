================================================================================
SUMMARY & ADVANTAGES OF PROMPT TEMPLATING
================================================================================
1. Elimination of Inline Drift: Prompt wording and grounding rules are maintained in `src/prompt_templates.py` rather than duplicated across files.
2. Multi-Feature Consistency: Both interactive RAG chat (`src/main.py`) and batch evaluation/CLI pipelines (`src/prompt_comparison.py`) consume identical template structures.
3. Safe Maintenance & Versioning: Modifying a grounding constraint in `src/prompt_templates.py` instantly updates all application features without touching business logic.
